NRevive

Our project aims to develop a state-of-the-art web application tailored for healthcare providers managing coma patients. Leveraging Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques, it analyzes medical notes to offer insights into coma severity, recovery prediction, and potential complications. Our platform revolutionizes coma patient care by providing actionable data and enhancing decision-making for healthcare professionals.
